# Code of Conduct

We want Telmoni's community to be a place anyone can take part in without being harassed, whoever they are and wherever they come from. This is one document for every Telmoni repository.

## What we expect

- Be kind and direct. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Accept feedback, and give it so that it can be accepted.
- When you get it wrong, say so, fix it and move on.

## What we do not accept

Harassment of any kind, in public or in private: insults, slurs, sexual attention, threats, stalking, publishing someone's private details, sustained disruption, or discrimination on any ground. Behaviour that would be unacceptable in a professional setting is unacceptable here.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project happens: the repositories, issues, pull requests, and anywhere someone is representing Telmoni.

## Reporting

Write to **hello@telmoni.com**. Every report is read, the reporter's identity is kept confidential, and you will get an answer. Depending on the behaviour, the response is a warning, a temporary exclusion or a permanent one; a maintainer who crosses the line is held to a higher standard, not a lower one.

Security problems are a different channel: see the [security policy](https://github.com/telmoni/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

_Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) and the [Rust Code of Conduct](https://www.rust-lang.org/policies/code-of-conduct)._
