# Support

Most answers are in the documentation at [docs.telmoni.com](https://docs.telmoni.com): self-hosting, the console, the CLI and SDKs, and the API, with what each [error](https://docs.telmoni.com/errors/) means. If it does not cover what you need:

- **A bug or a feature request:** open an issue in the repository concerned: [`telmoni/telmoni`](https://github.com/telmoni/telmoni/issues) for the server, the console and self-hosting; [`telmoni/telmoni-cli`](https://github.com/telmoni/telmoni-cli/issues) for the CLI and SDKs; [`telmoni/docs`](https://github.com/telmoni/docs/issues) for the documentation; [`telmoni/skills`](https://github.com/telmoni/skills/issues) for the agent skills.
- **A question the documentation does not answer:** open an issue on [`telmoni/telmoni`](https://github.com/telmoni/telmoni/issues), whatever part of Telmoni it is about.
- **Your account or an organization on telmoni.com:** write to hello@telmoni.com, and keep account details out of public issues.
- **A self-hosted deployment:** whoever runs it is your contact for accounts and access. Its console's footer and its emails give their address if they set one.
- **A suspected vulnerability:** never in public. Follow the [security policy](https://docs.telmoni.com/legal/security/#reporting-a-vulnerability).
